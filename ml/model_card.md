# AttendSmart — Attendance Risk Model Card

## 1. Intended use
Predicts, at the mid-point of a 10-week module (after session 12 of 20), whether a
participant is likely to fall below **75% attendance** in the second half of the
term — an early-warning signal for advisors/faculty to reach out, **not** an
automated grading, penalty, or eligibility decision.

## 2. Model
- Algorithm: Logistic Regression (`scikit-learn`), `StandardScaler` + `class_weight="balanced"`, `C=0.5`.
- Why logistic regression: with only 39 training rows, an interpretable linear
  model with visible coefficients is preferable to a black-box ensemble — every
  prediction in the app can be explained as "this student is flagged mainly
  because of X and Y," which matters for a human-reviewed, high-stakes-adjacent
  decision.
- Equivalent Azure ML building block: **Two-Class Logistic Regression** in
  Designer, or an AutoML classification job with the same feature columns
  (see `docs/deployment_guide.md`).

## 3. Training data
- **100% synthetic demo data.** Only the 39 participant *names* are real
  (from the CDAIO Campus Immersion attendee list, deduplicated); every
  attendance record, quiz score, LMS login count, and risk outcome was
  randomly generated by `data/generate_dataset.py` for this capstone.
- Do not present any number in this dataset as a real attendance or
  performance record.
- Label design deliberately avoids leakage: features come **only** from
  sessions 1-12 (weeks 1-6); the label is the *actual outcome* observed in
  sessions 13-20 (weeks 7-10). This mirrors a genuine forecasting task
  rather than restating the past.

## 4. Features used (7)
| Feature | Direction | Intuition |
|---|---|---|
| `consecutive_absences_early` | + risk | Longest absence streak in weeks 1-6 |
| `attendance_trend_early` | + risk | Attendance declining within weeks 1-6 |
| `late_arrival_pct_early` | mixed | Share of late arrivals |
| `avg_quiz_score_early` | − risk | Average quiz score, weeks 1-6 |
| `assignment_submission_rate_early` | mixed | Share of assignments submitted |
| `avg_lms_logins_early` | − risk | Weekly LMS login average |
| `avg_forum_posts_early` | − risk | Weekly forum/discussion posts |

Two candidate features were **deliberately excluded**: `attendance_pct_early`
(0.84-correlated with `consecutive_absences_early`, causing unstable/flipped
coefficients at this sample size) and `prior_module_score_pct` (~0
correlation with the outcome in this cohort). This is a documented modeling
choice, not an oversight.

## 5. Performance (Leave-One-Out cross-validation, n=39)
- Accuracy: **0.615** | Precision: **0.611** | Recall: **0.579** | F1: **0.595** | ROC-AUC: **0.750**
- LOO-CV was used instead of a train/test split because a single split on 39
  rows would be too small to trust; LOO gives every student a turn as the
  held-out test case.
- **Honest limitation:** at n=39, this is a genuinely small dataset, and two
  of the seven coefficients (`assignment_submission_rate_early`,
  `attendance_trend_early`) show a sign that is less stable than the others
  under resampling. The model is fit for a *classroom-scale, human-reviewed
  early-warning demo*, not for unsupervised, high-stakes use. A production
  version would need a much larger, multi-cohort dataset before its
  coefficients should be trusted individually (see `docs/deployment_guide.md`
  and the CDAIO discussion notes for how this would be scaled and monitored).

## 6. Fairness & governance notes
- No protected attributes (gender, caste, religion, disability, etc.) are
  used as features by design.
- `city` and `work_experience_years` are collected in the dataset for context
  but are **not** used as model inputs, to avoid encoding socioeconomic or
  geographic proxies into the risk score.
- Every prediction is shown with its top contributing factors (see the app's
  "why flagged" panel) so a human reviewer can sanity-check it before acting.
- All predictions are advisory: the app requires a human override/acknowledge
  step before a risk flag is treated as actionable (see governance features).

## 7. Retraining & monitoring
- Retrain each term with that term's own early-window data; do not reuse a
  prior cohort's coefficients across programs without re-validation.
- Track real-world precision/recall once outcomes are known each term, and
  recalibrate the 75% threshold and feature set if the false-positive rate
  (students flagged who were fine) is high enough to erode advisor trust in
  the tool.
